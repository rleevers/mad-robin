# Mad Robin Website - Research Report

## What Makes a Great Ceilidh Band Website

Research based on analysis of 15+ real ceilidh band websites across the UK and Ireland, plus best-practice guides for band/wedding vendor websites.

### Top-tier examples worth studying:

| Band | URL | What they nail |
|------|-----|----------------|
| **Licence to Ceilidh** | licencetoceilidh.co.uk | 100+ Google reviews, award badges, TV credits (BBC, C4), regional landing pages, upsell options |

### Key patterns from the best sites:

- **Social proof everywhere** - not just on a testimonials page, but woven throughout
- **Transparent pricing** - at minimum a "from GBP X" figure
- **Clear packages** - what's included (sets, caller, PA, disco, travel)
- **Multiple contact methods** - form + phone + email
- **"What is a ceilidh?" content** - essential for English audiences (huge SEO value for Exeter)

## 2. What Doesn't Work

### Common mistakes across ceilidh band websites:

- **No video** - in 2026, a band without video is at a severe disadvantage
- **Auto-playing music** - causes immediate bounce
- **Stale content** - "upcoming gigs" showing dates from 2023 signals abandonment
- **No mobile optimisation** - 63% of traffic is mobile; Google uses mobile-first indexing
- **No pricing indication** - creates friction; 78% of couples say price is their #1 factor
- **Generic stock imagery** instead of real band photos
- **Cluttered navigation** with too many menu items
- **Low-resolution or poorly lit photos** - instantly undermines credibility
- **Flash-era designs** - busy backgrounds, small fonts, outdated layouts
- **Email-only contact** with no response time commitment

## 3. What Mad Robin's Audience Actually Wants

### Wedding couples want:
- Video of a real ceilidh (actual guests dancing, not staged)
- Testimonials from other couples (easy to work with, caller engaged everyone)
- Clear package info (what's included, how long, what happens during breaks)
- Dance/song list
- First dance options
- Logistics (space needed, arrival time, parking, feeding)

### Corporate/event planners want:
- Professional credentials (public liability insurance, PAT-tested equipment)
- Technical spec (stage plot, power requirements, PA specs)
- Scalability (can the band size up/down?)
- References from similar events

### All bookers want:
- **Proof of quality** - reviews, awards, venue history
- **Pricing ballpark** - so they can budget
- **Fast response** - quick replies signal professionalism
- **Insurance & reliability** - assurance the band will show up

## 5. Design Direction: Warm & Rustic

### Colour palette:
- Dark earthy backgrounds (deep charcoal, dark forest green, or warm dark brown)
- Warm accent colours (amber/gold, terracotta, copper)
- Cream/off-white for text and contrast
- Three-colour rule: one main, one secondary, one accent

### Typography:
- Clean sans-serif for body text (readability first)
- Characterful serif or hand-drawn font for headings (folk personality)
- Large, bold hero text

### Imagery style:
- 70-80% candid/action shots (packed dance floors, spinning dancers, laughing guests)
- 20-30% professional band portraits
- Warm colour grading to match the rustic theme
- Venue variety (barns, halls, outdoor, marquees)

### Layout:
- Single-page scrolling homepage that previews all key sections
- Prominent CTA on every page ("Check Our Availability")
- Testimonials sprinkled throughout, not just on one page
- Mobile-first design

## 6. Conversion Essentials

### Above the fold:
- Single, clear CTA ("Check Availability for Your Event")
- Hero video or powerful action shot
- One-line value proposition

### Enquiry form (streamlined):
- Name, email, event date, event type, venue/location, message
- Optional: guest count, caller preference
- Accessible from every page (persistent button or footer form)

### Trust signals to include:
- Google review score + count
- Any awards or media mentions
- Number of events performed
- Testimonial quotes near CTAs
- "Response within 24 hours" promise

### Pricing approach:
- Show starting prices (e.g., "from GBP X for a 2-hour ceilidh")
- Tiered packages if applicable
- "Get a tailored quote" for custom events

## 8. Technical Recommendations

### Technology stack:
- **Astro** (static site generator) - ships zero JavaScript by default, blazing fast, excellent SEO
- **Decap CMS** (formerly Netlify CMS) - free, git-based CMS for content updates without code
- **Netlify** or **Cloudflare Pages** - free hosting tier, automatic SSL, CDN
- **YouTube embeds** for video, **SoundCloud** for audio
- **Formspree** or **Netlify Forms** for contact form (free tier)

### Why this stack:
- **Free hosting** (meets budget requirement)
- **CMS for updates** (events, testimonials, photos without touching code)
- **Fastest possible page loads** (static HTML, no server, CDN)
- **Best possible SEO** (Core Web Vitals scores, mobile-first, schema markup)
- **No maintenance burden** (no WordPress updates, no security patches, no database)

### Mobile priorities:
- Under 3-second page load
- Tap-friendly CTAs (minimum 44x44px touch targets)
- Click-to-call phone number
- Swipeable image galleries
- Native date picker on enquiry form

## 9. Differentiation Opportunities for Mad Robin

Based on the competitive landscape, here's how Mad Robin can stand out:

1. **"What is a Ceilidh?" hub** - Essential for the Southwest England market where ceilidh is less culturally embedded than in Scotland. This content alone could drive significant organic traffic.

2. **Public seasonal ceilidh events** - Like Fiddle Paradiddle's "Pop-up Ceilidhs", this is the ultimate "try before you buy" and builds local community. Feature these prominently.

3. **Caller as a feature** - Many bands include a caller but don't sell it. Position the caller as a key part of the experience ("Everyone dances, no experience needed").

4. **Devon/Southwest focus** - Very few ceilidh bands target the Southwest specifically. Regional landing pages could dominate local search with minimal competition.

5. **Venue partnerships** - Blog content about specific Devon wedding venues builds SEO and relationships.
