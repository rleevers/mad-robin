Serena began learning the recorder at school, but she only got into the folk scene after joining the UoE Folk Society in 2021 and she's been playing for ceilidhs ever since! When not ceilidh-ing, she can be found singing in a choir, binding her own notebooks and coveting larger and larger recorders to add to her collection.
