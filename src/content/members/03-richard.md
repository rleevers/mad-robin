Richard picked up the fiddle at folk sessions in Plymouth in 2016 and has been causing trouble with it ever since. He has played with bands, orchestras, operas, and choirs, but always keeps finding his way back to English folk. When not playing, he can be found up a climbing wall, which is quieter (but arguably more dangerous).
