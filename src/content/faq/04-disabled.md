---
question: "What if I'm disabled?"
category: "Accessibility"
sortOrder: 4
---

Everyone is welcome on our dance floor, and ceilidh dancing is wonderfully adaptable. If you use a wheelchair and have the choice, we'd gently suggest a manual (non-motorised) one — it makes it easier for other dancers to take your hands and move with you through the figures. Most importantly, just let us know in advance: our caller will happily choose and adapt the dances so they work well for everyone taking part.
